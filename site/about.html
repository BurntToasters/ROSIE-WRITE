About ROSIE-WRITE

ROSIE-WRITE is a lightweight, browser-based note-taking application designed for simplicity and ease of use. It runs entirely in your browser and saves all your notes locally within your browser's local storage.

Features

  • Clean, document-focused interface.
  • Rich text editing powered by a modern editor engine.
  • Autosave — your work is saved automatically in the background.
  • Export to HTML, Markdown, plain text, PDF, and RTF.

Privacy First

Your notes are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage. This means:

  • Your note data never leaves your computer.
  • No tracking or analytics on your content. Your content is your content.
  • Open source! Check out the source code here on GitHub.

More Info

ROSIE-WRITE uses Cloudflare to deliver the web app and cache content, decreasing load on the origin. Cloudflare analytics are also in use to preserve user privacy while allowing us to see the amount of visitors to the site.

"Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use any client-side state, such as cookies or localStorage, to collect usage metrics. We also don't 'fingerprint' individuals via their IP address, User Agent string, or any other data for the purpose of displaying analytics. Our analytics are non-invasive and respect the privacy of your visitors."

— Cloudflare

Getting Started

Using ROSIE-WRITE is simple — just start typing! Your notes are automatically saved as you write. You can create multiple notes, format your text with the toolbar, and export your work when you're ready to share it.
